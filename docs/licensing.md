# License decision: MIT

The project owner selected MIT on 2026-11-13. The authoritative terms are in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).

Glimpse is intended to remain fully open source. MIT makes its code easy to reuse in other utilities, reduces license compatibility friction for adopters, and allows another maintainer to continue it if this project becomes inactive. Those are product choices, not guarantees of adoption or future maintenance.

MIT allows commercial use and closed-source derivatives, provided its copyright and permission notice requirements are met. Our commitment to publish this project does not oblige other distributors to disclose their changes. That is the principal trade-off.

GPLv3 would instead preserve source access for recipients of distributed covered derivatives and require those derivatives to use its terms. It permits commercial sales and private modifications; it does not require contributors to submit patches upstream. It would be the better choice if preventing closed distributed derivatives were the primary objective. Both licenses allow others to fork and maintain the application.

iGlance is GPL-licensed. Studying its behaviour and architecture is different from copying or adapting its protected implementation. This project starts independently: do not transplant iGlance code into an MIT-only codebase without a compatible licensing arrangement. Record the origin and license of any imported component and retain required notices. MIT licensing of our original work does not override third-party terms.
