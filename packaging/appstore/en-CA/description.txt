Glimpse puts your Mac's vital signs in one small menu-bar icon. Read CPU, memory, network, battery, disk and thermal state at a glance; click for exact values and short histories.

• One to eight meters, in the order and colours you choose
• Warning colours when a reading crosses your threshold
• Upload and download for each network interface, such as Wi-Fi or Ethernet
• Battery health, cycles and time remaining
• A detailed panel you arrange yourself, and an optional keyboard shortcut

Native and light: Glimpse reads the system's own counters and pauses while your Mac sleeps. No account, no analytics; your readings never leave your Mac.

Every feature is free. Optional tips are in Settings › About.
