Glimpse puts your Mac's vital signs in one small menu-bar icon: a row of slim meters you can read at a glance. Click it for exact values, short histories and network details.

WHAT IT SHOWS
• CPU activity, with per-core bars in the panel
• Memory in use
• Network upload and download, separately or as one split meter, for the interface you choose (Wi-Fi, Ethernet or a VPN)
• Battery charge, time remaining, health and cycle count
• Free disk space
• Thermal state

MAKE IT YOURS
• One to eight meters, in any order and colour
• Warning and critical thresholds that recolour a meter when it matters
• Monochrome mode for a quieter menu bar
• A detailed panel of cards you arrange and size yourself
• An optional keyboard shortcut to show or hide the panel from any app
• Bits or bytes, decimal (GB) or binary (GiB) units

LIGHT ON YOUR MAC
Glimpse is a native app that reads the system's own counters. It samples only what is on screen, pauses while your Mac sleeps, and redraws the icon only when something visibly changes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your readings never leave your Mac.

FREE
Every feature is free. If Glimpse is useful to you, you can leave an optional tip in Settings › About.
